# Production Quality Checklist

Use this checklist before final delivery.

## Story and research
- [ ] The opening hook is clear within the first 1–3 seconds when appropriate.
- [ ] The video has a coherent beginning, middle, and ending.
- [ ] Factual claims have traceable sources.
- [ ] Dates, names, units, numbers, and captions are checked.
- [ ] The script fits the target duration naturally.

## Visual design
- [ ] One consistent visual system is used throughout.
- [ ] Every scene has a clear focal point.
- [ ] Text is readable on a phone at the actual output size.
- [ ] No unintended clipping, stretching, overlap, or off-screen elements.
- [ ] Generated characters, products, and environments remain consistent.
- [ ] Image crops preserve the important subject.
- [ ] Effects support the message and do not create clutter.
- [ ] Transitions feel intentional and correctly timed.

## Animation
- [ ] Motion is frame-driven and deterministic.
- [ ] Keyframes begin and end at intended frames.
- [ ] Easing is deliberate; no accidental linear drift or jitter.
- [ ] No unexplained frozen frames or timing gaps.
- [ ] Important text remains on screen long enough to read.
- [ ] Scene duration and total composition duration agree.

## Audio
- [ ] Narration is clear and synchronized.
- [ ] Music is below narration.
- [ ] SFX are selective and correctly timed.
- [ ] No clipping, abrupt volume changes, or missing audio.
- [ ] Captions match the spoken words.

## Render
- [ ] Preview was inspected at the target aspect ratio.
- [ ] Opening, middle, ending, and complex transitions were checked.
- [ ] Final output file exists and is playable.
- [ ] Actual resolution, fps, and duration were verified where possible.
- [ ] Source project and asset manifest are included.
